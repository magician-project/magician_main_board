MCP23018
Port GPA0 is connected to Push Button 0 (PB0) Input
Port GPA1 is connected to Push Button 1 (PB1) Input
Port GPA2 is connected to ETH Reset pin (Output)
Port GPA3 is connected to XSHUT/laserSwitch port 1 (Output)
Port GPA4 is connected to XSHUT/laserSwitch port 2 (Output)
Port GPA5 is connected to XSHUT/laserSwitch port 3 (Output)
Port GPA6 is connected to A 5V Rail Enable pin (Output) (This will be inverted in 1.4)
Port GPA7 is connected to A 11.5V Rail Enable pin (Output) (This will be inverted in 1.4)

Port GPB0 is connected to nothing
Port GPB1 is connected to nothing 
Port GPB2 is connected to nothing 
Port GPB3 is connected to nothing
Port GPB4 is connected to nothing
Port GPB5 is connected to nothing
Port GPB6 is connected to nothing
Port GPB7 is connected to nothing


The initialization of the MCP23018 must be:
GPA0 is an input button
GPA1 is an input button
GPA2 is an output and should be High
GPA3,GPA4,GPA5 are outputs and should be High
GPA6 is an output should also be High 
GPA7 is an output should also be High


Ports GP4 and GP5 are connected to 3x ToF I2C sensors with initial address 0x52
The address of the MCP23018 is 0x20


The XCG-CP510 Camera is emitting an Active High exposure signal to Port GP2 of the Pico 2

The exposure signal enables a specific light.
Lights are connected in the following way.

Light 1 is connected to GP6
Light 2 is connected to GP7
Light 3 is connected to GP8
Light 4 is connected to GP9
Light 5 is connected to GP10
Light 6 is connected to GP11

The ethernet (W5500 USR-ES1)
is connected in the following way:
MISO is connected to GP16 of the Pico2
MOSI is connected to GP19 of the Pico2
SCCLK is connected to GP18 of the Pico2
SCN is connected to GP17 of the Pico2
INT is connected to GP22 of the Pico2
RST is connected to GPA2 of the MCP23018
